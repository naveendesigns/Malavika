01 · INTRO

what's up?

I'm Malavika. I started in healthcare operations and became increasingly interested in the products and workflows behind the work.

Working inside high-volume healthcare workflows taught me how quickly a small process problem can become a much bigger operational problem.

Eventually, I stopped looking only at the task in front of me and started asking:

  • Why is this taking so long?
  • Where are people getting stuck?
  • What information are they missing?
  • Can the workflow itself be better?

That curiosity pulled me toward data, root-cause analysis and cross-functional problem solving, and eventually into product management.
