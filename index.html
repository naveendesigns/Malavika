01 · INTRO

what's up?

I'm Malavika, a healthcare operations professional who slowly became obsessed with the products behind the processes.

I've spent 3+ years inside high-volume healthcare workflows, where one small process problem can quietly turn into hundreds of unresolved claims.

Over time, I stopped looking only at the problem in front of me and started asking bigger, more annoying questions:

  • Why is this taking so long?
  • Where are people getting stuck?
  • What information are they missing?
  • Can the workflow itself be better?

That curiosity pulled me toward data, root-cause analysis and cross-functional collaboration, and eventually toward product thinking.
