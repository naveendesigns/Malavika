Turning a study abroad brief into a real product

I took a broad study abroad consultancy brief and worked backwards from the real problem: helping counsellors know which students need attention, what is blocking them, and what should happen next.

question I kept coming back to:

who needs my attention right now?

02 · THE QUICK READ

the whole thing,
four cards

Problem

Counsellors manage students across multiple stages, documents, applications and follow-ups, but the workflow can become fragmented.

User

Counsellors and operations teams who need a clear view of student progress and next actions.

Product opportunity

Create one operational view that turns student progress into clear next actions.

Product principle

Don't make counsellors search for the next action. Make the next action obvious.

03 · THE BUSINESS CONTEXT

one journey,
five departments

A study abroad consultancy isn't one process. It's nine stages of a student's life, worked by five different teams. The student experiences it as one continuous journey. The organisation experiences it as a series of handoffs.

That gap is where things slow down, and it's the reason a generic CRM never quite fixes it.

04 · THE REAL PROBLEM

not "we need a CRM"

  1. WHO NEEDS MY ATTENTION?
  2. WHAT IS BLOCKING THEM?
  3. WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN NEXT?

The problem was not simply storing student information. The bigger problem was helping the team understand what matters now.

SECONDARY

SECONDARY

Finance

SECONDARY

Admin

Needs the pipeline view: volumes, productivity, drop-offs.

SECONDARY

Operations

Keeps the machine running day to day, across every stage.

the product is not trying to replace the team's workflow. it is trying to make the workflow visible →

15 · EDGE CASES

edge cases I would design for

01 · Multiple applications+

SITUATION

One student can have multiple universities, each with a different status.

PRODUCT RESPONSE

Keep applications separate while keeping the student as the primary entity.

02 · Rejected document+

SITUATION

A document can be uploaded but still be unusable.

PRODUCT RESPONSE

Show the reason, owner and next action instead of simply showing "Rejected."

03 · Counsellor ownership changes+

SITUATION

A student may move between counsellors.

PRODUCT RESPONSE

Preserve history while making current ownership obvious.

16 · METRICS

how I would know
it is working

PROPOSED METRICS

TIME TO FIRST ACTION

  • How quickly a counsellor can identify and act on a student needing attention.

OVERDUE ACTION RATE

  • Percentage of student actions that remain overdue.

DOCUMENT RESOLUTION TIME

  • Time from a document being rejected or missing to successfully verified.

STUDENT PROGRESS RATE

  • Percentage of active students moving to their next stage within the expected timeframe.

nothing here is a result · these are the numbers I'd want on the wall →

19 · NEXT

what I would validate next

Research questions, not established findings. The answers could reshape the MVP, and that would be product discovery doing its job.

THE WORK

  • Which student information do counsellors check most frequently?
  • How do counsellors currently decide who needs attention?
  • Which part of the workflow creates the most manual work?

HANDOFFS & OWNERSHIP

  • Where do handoffs fail most often?
  • How often does ownership change?

DOCUMENTS

  • What makes a document "ready" for submission?

MEASUREMENT

  • How are overdue actions currently tracked?
  • Which metrics does leadership already use?

20 · THE THESIS

A-WAY should not become another place where counsellors store information.

It should become the place where they understand what needs to happen next.

FROMSTUDENT INFORMATION TOSTUDENT PROGRESS TOCLEAR NEXT ACTION

that's the product I would build first.
