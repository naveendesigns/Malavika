04 · THE PROBLEM

not a need problem.
a delivery problem.

01

Discovery barrier

Most users don't know TM Caf exists. It's buried under the dating interface.

02

Moment-match barrier

Users aren't routed into Caf at the emotional moment when support is most relevant.

03

Supply-trust barrier

Users can't quickly decide which expert is right for them.

04

Monetization risk

Poor packaging could cause Caf to cannibalize existing dating subscriptions.

The opportunity is not to create a new need. It is to connect an existing need with the right product experience.
